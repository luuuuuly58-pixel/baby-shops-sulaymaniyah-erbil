# Baby Shops — Sulaymaniyah & Erbil

A contact list of baby-product shops, chains, distributors and pharmacies in
Sulaymaniyah and Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq — built for phone use, with
tappable call, WhatsApp, Instagram and map links.

**Live page:** https://luuuuuly58-pixel.github.io/baby-shops-sulaymaniyah-erbil/

## What's here

| | |
|---|---|

| Shops in Sulaymaniyah | 50 |
| Other Sulaymaniyah Governorate towns | 9 |

Available in **English** and **Kurdish (Sorani)** — switch with the button in the
top corner. Also downloadable as PDF (both languages) and CSV.

## Notes on the data

- Phone numbers are shown in local Iraqi format. Call and WhatsApp links use the
  international form (+964, leading zero dropped).
- Numbers were correct when collected. **Confirm on the call** — contact details
  in this market change often.
- Where no number is listed, none was published. Those shops are reachable by
  Instagram, Facebook or a walk-in. Nothing was invented to fill a gap.
- The pharmacy list is **unscreened**: pharmacies commonly sell formula and
  diapers, but stocking varies. Ring to check before pitching.

## Sources

OpenStreetMap (via the Overpass API) for shop locations and some phone numbers;
OSM Nominatim for street addresses; the [Awata Iraq business
directory](https://iqbd.yocat.net) (Sulaymaniyah Governorate only — it has no
Erbil records); company websites; and the shops' own Facebook and Instagram
pages.

Erbil and Sulaymaniyah were researched by different routes because Sulaymaniyah
has a working online directory and Erbil does not. That is a difference in how
findable the two cities are online, not a difference in market size.
